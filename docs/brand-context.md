# Khel Shiksha — Brand & Content Context

Source: the two-sided three-fold print company profile in `assets/`. This file is the
extracted reference for building the website.

## Identity

- **Name:** Khel Shiksha (logo sets it as "Khel" over "Shiksha", registered ®)
- **Tagline:** Build • Play • Learn
- **Secondary line:** "Learning Through Play"
- **What it is:** A gamified, experiential learning ecosystem for Vidyalayas (schools) —
  physical educational game kits plus training, aimed at Indian primary/middle school children.

## Vision

To democratize joy-based, experiential learning for every child, transforming classrooms into
vibrant hubs of discovery.

## Mission

1. Empowering students through activity-based learning aligned with NEP 2020.
2. Fostering critical thinking, empathy, and life-long curiosity.
3. Bridging the gap between conceptual knowledge and real-world application.

## Why Experiential Learning?

Positioning line: **"Shifting from rote memorization to Learning by Doing."**

1. Understand concepts faster
2. Build confidence through participation
3. Improve teamwork and communication
4. Strengthen problem-solving ability
5. Connect learning with real life

## Visual language

- **Background:** warm cream / ivory, with soft organic blob shapes in pale pink, peach, and blue
- **Primary:** a friendly mid-blue (logo, headings, accents)
- **Accents:** magenta/pink pill badges, red, green, orange
- **Cards:** pastel tints (pink, blue, mint, peach, lavender) with wavy/scalloped edges
- **Type:** bold sans-serif headings with italic serif-ish emphasis on key phrases
  ("_Learning by Doing._", "_Holistic Development_", "_Credibility_")
- **Imagery:** 3D-rendered cartoon children with books, dice, and a globe; playful, rounded, high-gloss
- **Overall feel:** bright, childlike, optimistic — but paired with government/institutional
  credibility markers
